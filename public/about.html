About DomainCheck

Welcome to DomainCheck, a lightweight, fast, and high-accuracy domain availability search engine operated by Ujwal Web Development and Creation.

Our Mission

Our goal is to provide entrepreneurs, students, web developers, and creators with a secure, reliable, and completely free domain lookup tool without requiring user registration, subscription fees, or invasive personal tracking.

How Our Lookup Engine Works

Unlike standard DNS checkers that only verify active IP addresses (A records), DomainCheck integrates directly with global RDAP (Registration Data Access Protocol) servers provided by central domain registries (such as Verisign and Public Interest Registry).

This ensures that even if a domain is parked or lacks an active website, DomainCheck accurately identifies its registration status in real time.

Independent Web Project

DomainCheck is an independent informational technology tool. We do not act as a domain registrar, nor do we reserve, hoard, or sell domain names directly. All registry lookups are performed anonymously for research purposes.
