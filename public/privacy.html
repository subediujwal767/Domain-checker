Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

At DomainCheck (accessible from https://domain-checker-oh01.onrender.com), operated by Ujwal Web Development and Creation, visitor privacy is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by DomainCheck and how we use it.

1. Search Queries & Data Privacy

DomainCheck is designed as an anonymous lookup engine. We do not save, store, or log any domain names entered into our domain availability checker. Your search queries are sent directly and temporarily to public registry APIs solely to process your live lookup request.

2. Google AdSense & Cookie Disclosures

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense to support site maintenance. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Log Files

DomainCheck follows standard procedures of using log files provided by hosting services (Render). These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under European GDPR and California CCPA guidelines, users have the right to request information regarding data usage. Because DomainCheck does not collect personal user profiles, accounts, or stored personal data, no user tracking database exists to sell, process, or share.
